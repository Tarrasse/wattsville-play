Where power began

Survey grid, 10° squares · the cable joins the cities you played, in date order

How to play

  • Each city makes its power one way: water, coal, wind, nuclear or gas. Each kind of plant has a rule for where it can stand. Drag the Plant tile onto the map: a green square shows where a plant can stand.
  • Drag from a plant, home or cable to lay cable ($1 a square, $8 more over water). Erase cable to get the money back.
  • Each plant powers as many homes as its pips. Too many homes on one network share the power: they all dim and pay less. Hold a plant to add 2 pips, or tap it to see its week, upgrade it or sell it. A plant takes 3 upgrades at most; after that, build a new plant.
  • Each color is a voltage for one kind of customer: amber for homes, blue for factories, magenta for tram depots, green for charging stations. Factories and depots use 2 pips but pay more.
  • Each lit home pays $30 a week; with every home lit, pay is ×1.25. Each week ends with bills for cable, plants and fuel. The chip next to your cash shows your profit a week: tap it for the details. It glows when a week ends: tap it to see the week's statement.
  • The town has a size limit. When it has no more room, it grows up: a home becomes an apartment block, then a tower block, and other customers get more floors. Each level needs more pips and pays more. Scaffolding and a + show over a building for a few seconds before it rises: add capacity to its network, or it browns out.
  • Stars count your profit: your best cash, less the start cash. Unlit homes slowly dim; if one goes fully dark, the game ends.
  • The efficiency medal (bronze, silver or gold) shows what your customers paid for each $1 of bills and building, and how many stayed lit. Spare pips and unused cable lower it. It does not change the stars.
